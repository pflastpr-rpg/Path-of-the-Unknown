Build Guide & Mechanics Reference

"Know the numbers. Master the battlefield."

This reference compiles every core calculation in Path of the Unknown — how To-Hit rolls are built, how wounds are calculated, how defense is structured, and what skills and characteristics feed into each. Use this as your roadmap when building or advancing a character.

The Core Roll

Almost every action in the game resolves with a single d10 roll compared against a Difficulty Value (DV). Roll equal to or above the DV to succeed.

ROLL: 1d10 + Relevant Characteristic ≥ DV
  • Natural 10 — Always a Critical Success regardless of DV. Triggers bonus effects on most skills.
  • Natural 1 — Always a failure. Some skills apply additional penalties.
  • Advantage — Roll 2d10, take the higher result.
  • Disadvantage — Roll 2d10, take the lower result.
Example: You attempt a Melee strike (DV 6). Your Agility is 4. You roll a 3 on the d10. Total = 3 + 4 = 7. Success — the blow lands.

Action Points (AP)

Each combatant receives 3 Action Points (AP) per turn. Every action has an AP cost. When your pool is empty, your turn ends.

  • 1 AP — Standard attack, move, use an item, reactionary skill (Block, Deflect, Dodge)
  • 2 AP — Most Advanced Vocation activations, power stances, Jailbreak escape
  • 3 AP — Heavy commitment strikes (Whirlwind, Full-Weight, Slash, Lights-Out)
  • 5 AP (Sequential) — Epic Vocation abilities like Rebirth (spread across consecutive turns)

Reactionary skills (Block, Deflect, Dodge) are spent from your next turn's pool when triggered by an enemy attack during their turn.

Build Tip: Characters who want to use 2 AP skills offensively and still have a reactionary defense should plan around skills that refresh AP (Command's Blood Rally crit) or that eliminate the need for reactions entirely (Battle-Ready, high Dodge).

Melee To-Hit

Melee

Melee To-Hit is a contested or DV-based roll. Your primary offensive characteristic is Agility, though some skills also feed in Physique or Awareness.

BASE MELEE TO-HIT: 1d10 + Agility vs. Target DV

What raises your Melee To-Hit:

  • Agility Characteristic — Direct bonus to every melee roll.
  • Melee Weapon Skill Points — Skill ranks grant additional flat bonuses as defined by the weapon proficiency.
  • Battle-Ready (Berserker spec) — Ignores all Disadvantage penalties to To-Hit for that turn.
  • Nimble Strike — Advantage on the To-Hit roll when using the skill.
  • Phantom Assassin — Advantage To-Hit when attacking from Stealth or while Flanking.
  • Shield Rush — Bonus To-Hit equal to your Shieldsman Skill Points.
  • Expose Armor — Does not improve To-Hit, but lowers effective WM on a hit (see Wound Mitigation).
  • Command (Blood Rally crit) — Grants an ally a refreshed AP pool, enabling an additional attack.

⚔ Melee To-Hit Build Path

  • Core stat: Agility — maximize first
  • Weapon Proficiency ranks for flat To-Hit bonuses
  • Add Nimble Strike for Advantage on precision strikes
  • Add Phantom Assassin if playing a flanker/stealth build — Advantage + Wound bonus
  • Add Battle-Ready if fighting multiple opponents to negate their flanking penalties on your rolls
  • Add Shield Rush for a guaranteed To-Hit bonus on charge attacks (requires Shieldsman ranks)

Ranged To-Hit

Ranged

Ranged To-Hit uses Awareness as the primary characteristic. Ranged attacks suffer penalties for obscured targets or allies in the line of fire unless countered.

BASE RANGED TO-HIT: 1d10 + Awareness vs. Target DV

What raises your Ranged To-Hit:

  • Awareness Characteristic — Direct bonus to every ranged roll.
  • Ranged Weapon Skill Points — Ranks in your weapon type (Bow, Crossbow, Blackpowder) add flat bonuses.
  • Opportunist — Removes all negative penalties for shooting into melee or with obscured Line of Sight.
  • Marksman (Sniper spec) — Advantage To-Hit when using the Sniper specialization.
  • Heavy Arbalest (Linebreaker spec) — Gains Advantage To-Hit during the Linebreaker specialization.
  • Thunderstrike (Blackpowder) — Does not modify To-Hit but every successful hit adds Stunned conditions, effectively pressuring target reactions.

🏹 Ranged To-Hit Build Path

  • Core stat: Awareness — maximize first
  • Ranged Weapon Proficiency ranks for flat To-Hit bonuses
  • Add Opportunist to eliminate shooting-into-melee penalties entirely
  • Add Marksman → Sniper for Advantage on 2-AP focused shots
  • Add Heavy Arbalest → Linebreaker for Advantage plus pierce-through capability
  • Secondary stat: Agility — feeds into Sniper wound formula

Spell To-Cast

Spell

Casting uses Intellect (arcane) or Faith / Willpower (divine/nature) as the primary characteristic depending on your magic school. Each spell has a Target Number (TN) you must meet or beat.

BASE SPELL TO-CAST: 1d10 + Magic Skill ≥ Spell TN

What raises your Spell To-Cast:

  • Magic Skill Ranks (Wizardry, Faith, Druidism etc.) — Direct bonus to casting rolls.
  • Intellect / Faith / Willpower Characteristic — Depending on school, feeds into cast and wound calculations.
  • Concentrate — Grants Advantage on the To-Cast roll and boosts resulting wounds.
  • Deviation — Does not improve To-Cast but lets you redirect a successful cast to a different target mid-flight.
  • Metaphysical — Increases potency on successful TN ≤ 7 casts (does not modify To-Cast directly).
  • Infusion of Arcana — Converts an offensive spell into healing; requires Wizardry 7+.

✦ Spell To-Cast Build Path

  • Core skill: Maximize your magic school rank (Wizardry / Faith / Druidism)
  • Core stat: Intellect (arcane) or Faith / Willpower (divine/nature)
  • Add Concentrate for Advantage on To-Cast + permanent +3 Spell Wound bonus
  • Add Metaphysical for stacking passive wound/healing bonuses on lower-TN spells
  • Add Deviation → Divergence for multi-target flexibility without needing separate rolls
  • Add Concentrate → Intensify for catastrophic double-damage burst turns

Melee Wounds

Melee

Melee wound output stacks from several sources: base weapon damage, characteristic bonuses, skill bonuses, and advanced vocation modifiers.

MELEE WOUNDS = Weapon Damage + Physique Bonus + Skill Bonuses + Vocation Modifiers

Passive wound bonuses that stack:

  • Impact — Permanent +2 Melee Wound Bonus. 1H adds Physique; 2H adds 2×Physique.
  • Nimble Strike — Permanent +1 Melee Wound Bonus.
  • Expose Armor — Permanent +1 Melee Wound Bonus.
  • Phantom Assassin — +2 Melee Wound Bonus (Stealth/Flank only).
  • Throat Slice — Permanent +1 Melee Wound Bonus.
  • Impale — Permanent +1 Melee Wound Bonus.

Active wound formulas (selected examples):

  • Nimble Strike: Normal + Agility + Awareness
  • Impact (2H): Normal + (2 × Physique)
  • Impale (2H): Normal + 1d10 + 2 Bleeding
  • Throat Slice: Normal + 1d10 + (2 × Agility)
  • Tune-up: Normal + 1d10 + Physique + 2 Stunned
  • Cleave (Whirlwind): Normal + (2 × Physique) — hits all enemies in reach
Stack Example — Agility Striker: Nimble Strike (+1 passive) + Phantom Assassin (+2 from stealth) + Throat Slice (+1 passive) = +4 Melee Wound Bonus baseline before any roll. On a Nimble Strike activation: Normal + Agility + Awareness on top of that.

⚔ Melee Wound Build Paths

  • Agility Finisher: Agility + Awareness high → Nimble Strike, Phantom Assassin, Throat Slice, Impale
  • Physique Bruiser: Physique high → Impact (2H), Cleave, Sunder, Tune-up, Shield Rush
  • Hybrid Striker: Agility + Physique balanced → Nimble Strike + Impact + Expose Armor for consistent output

Ranged Wounds

Ranged
RANGED WOUNDS = Weapon Damage + Awareness Bonus + Skill Bonuses + Vocation Modifiers

Passive wound bonuses that stack:

  • Marksman — Permanent +2 Ranged Wound Bonus.
  • Opportunist — Permanent +1 Ranged Wound Bonus.
  • Heavy Arbalest — Permanent +1 Ranged Wound Bonus. All bolts ignore 2 pts Armor Mitigation.
  • Thunderstrike (Blackpowder) — Permanent +2 Ranged Wound Bonus.

Active wound formulas:

  • Marksman: Normal + Awareness + 1d10
  • Marksman (Sniper): Normal + Agility + (2 × Awareness) + 1d10 + 1 Bleeding
  • Opportunist (Seeker): Normal + 1d10 + (3 × Awareness) + Agility — Advantage To-Hit
  • Heavy Arbalest (Linebreaker): Normal + 2d10 + (2 × Awareness) — ignores all worn armor WM
  • Thunderstrike (Point-Blank): Normal + 3d10 + (2 × Awareness) + 2 Bleeding — cannot be parried

🏹 Ranged Wound Build Paths

  • Precision Archer: Awareness max → Marksman + Opportunist + Seeker spec (+4 passive, massive Sniper burst)
  • Arbalest Breaker: Awareness + Physique → Heavy Arbalest (armor ignore) + Impact for melee fallback
  • Blackpowder Brawler: Awareness high → Thunderstrike + Point-Blank Execution (unblockable, AOE stun)

Spell Wounds

Spell
SPELL WOUNDS = Spell Base + Intellect/Faith Bonus + Magic Skill Bonus + Vocation Modifiers

Passive wound bonuses that stack:

  • Concentrate — Permanent +3 Spell Wound Bonus.
  • Metaphysical — Permanent +1 Wound Bonus (offensive) and +1 Healing Bonus.
  • Divinity — Healing bonus equal to Faith Skill Points (healing spells TN ≤ 7).

Active wound formulas:

  • Concentrate: Normal + Intellect Bonus on success
  • Concentrate (Intensify spec): Total wounds × 2. Natural 10s chain additional d10 surges.
  • Metaphysical: Normal + Spirit Skill Points
  • Metaphysical (Supernatural spec): Normal + (3 × Spirit) + (2 × Willpower)
  • Deviation (Divergence spec): (Normal + 1d10) ÷ number of targets + full static bonuses each
  • Critical Resonance (Concentrate crit): Normal + 1d10 + (3 × Intellect Bonus)
Stack Example — Arcane Blaster: Concentrate (+3 passive) + Metaphysical (+1 passive) = +4 Spell Wound Bonus baseline. On a Concentrate activation: Normal + Intellect on top. On Intensify spec: double everything. On a crit: Normal + 1d10 + (3 × Intellect).

✦ Spell Wound Build Paths

  • Arcane Blaster: Intellect + Wizardry max → Concentrate + Metaphysical + Intensify spec for double-damage burst
  • Battlefield Caster: Intellect + Awareness → Deviation + Divergence spec to hit multiple targets in one action
  • Battle Healer: Faith + Willpower → Divinity + Metaphysical + Sanctity spec for massive single-target heals
  • Hybrid Arcane: Wizardry + Intellect → Infusion of Arcana to convert damage to healing on demand

Healing & Blessings

Heal
HEALING = Blessing Base + Faith/Willpower Bonus + Skill Bonuses + Vocation Modifiers
  • Divinity: Normal + Faith Skill Points (TN ≤ 7 healing spells). Crit: Normal + (2 × Faith).
  • Divinity (Sanctity spec): Normal + (2 × Faith) + 1d10. Removes Bleeding. Crit: Normal + (3 × Faith) + 2d10.
  • Metaphysical: Adds Spirit Skill Points to healing output.
  • Cleric (Vessel of Restoration passive): +1 additional Wound recovered whenever healed by a Blessing.
  • Rebirth (Epic): 1d10 + Willpower on automatic trigger at 0 Wounds. Seed of Life spec pulses (1d10 + Druidism Rank) AoE healing to all allies.
  • Infusion of Arcana: Converts offensive spell to 50% healing output. Crit = 100%.
  • Infusion (True Fusion spec): Full wounds on enemy AND 50% healing to ally in one cast.
  • Command (Blood Rally spec): 1d10 + (Charisma × 2) healing if target is at 0 Wounds.

💚 Healing Build Path

  • Core stats: Faith + Willpower
  • Divinity → Sanctity for single-target burst heal + Bleed removal
  • Metaphysical for stacking passive healing bonus
  • Cleric passive for bonus heal on every blessing received
  • Rebirth (Epic) for guaranteed 0-wound comeback (requires Druidism 12)
  • Infusion of Arcana for flexible damage/heal conversion (Wizardry path)

Wound Mitigation (WM)

Defense

Wound Mitigation reduces incoming damage after a hit lands. It stacks from armor, shields, skills, and characteristics.

WOUNDS TAKEN = Wounds Dealt − Total WM (minimum 0)

Sources that raise WM:

  • Worn Armor — Base WM from equipped gear.
  • Shield — Additional WM from equipped shield.
  • Block (Bulwark spec) — +5 WM to self and one adjacent ally for the stance duration.
  • Cleric (passive) — Permanent +1 WM against all damage.
  • Unwavering (Epic, passive) — Permanent +3 WM against all sources.
  • Sovereign Mind — Mitigates all incoming wounds by your Willpower characteristic for the turn.
  • Sovereign Mind (Indomitable spec) — Extra WM = (2 × Willpower) + Physique until next turn.
  • Primal Aspect (Guardian form) — +3 WM while shapeshifted.
  • Primal Aspect (Iron-Grown Chitin mutation) — +2 WM additional. Thorns damage on melee hits.
  • Cleric (Aegis of Light) — WM increased by Light Soul Points against "Evil" targets.
  • Rebirth (Seed of Life spec) — Allies gain +2 WM (Bramble Shield) after the resurrection pulse.

What reduces enemy WM (your offense):

  • Expose Armor: Reduces target WM by Awareness + 1d10 on a hit.
  • Expose Armor (Undress spec): Target gains zero WM from worn armor for that strike.
  • Heavy Arbalest: All bolts ignore 2 pts of armor WM passively.
  • Heavy Arbalest (Linebreaker spec): Ignores all worn armor WM entirely.
  • Sunder: Permanently reduces enemy shield WM by 1 per hit until repaired.
  • Tune-up (Lights-Out spec crit): Completely bypasses all WM.
  • Thunderstrike (Point-Blank): Cannot be parried or blocked by shields.

🛡 Tank / WM Build Path

  • Heavy armor base + shield for maximum passive WM
  • Block → Bulwark for +5 WM shared stance
  • Cleric passive +1 WM (requires Faith 3, Light Soul 2)
  • Unwavering (Epic) passive +3 WM (requires Hard as Nails 2, Iron Will 2, Willpower 2)
  • Sovereign Mind → Indomitable for massive single-turn WM spike
  • Unwavering → Unstoppable Force (Epic spec) to mitigate equal to Hard as Nails + Iron Will + Willpower combined

Defense & Avoidance

Defense

Beyond WM, characters can avoid damage entirely through active defense skills.

BLOCK: Negates all wounds from one melee hit (same size or smaller). Costs 1 AP.
DEFLECT: Opposed roll (Agility/Melee) to negate all wounds from one melee hit. Costs 1 AP.
DODGE: Declare after being hit. Attack becomes a total miss. No size restriction. Costs 1 AP.
  • Block — Full negate. Fails vs. Giants/Enormous/Titanic. Bulwark spec shares +5 WM with one ally.
  • Deflect — Opposed roll. Revert-Back spec forces attacker to take half their own wounds back.
  • Dodge — Total miss, universal (works vs. giants, arrows, magic). Jailbreak spec also breaks all melee engagements and moves you (Athletics × 5 ft).
  • Battle-Ready — Passive; negates flanking/outnumbering bonuses from up to 3 opponents. Not a reaction — always on while in the stance.
  • Sovereign Mind — Active WM spike + mental condition removal. Indomitable spec also grants immunity to Stunned and Prone.
Avoidance vs. Mitigation: Dodge is the safest universal option but costs AP. Block is free output negation but size-gated. WM stacking is passive but doesn't stop everything. Most tanky builds combine WM stacking with a Block reaction and a Dodge as emergency backup.

Conditions Reference

Conditions are applied by skills, crits, and environmental hazards. Many Advanced Vocations apply or interact with conditions.

  • Bleeding — Ongoing damage per turn. Stacks. Removed by healing skills (Divinity/Sanctity) or treatment.
  • Stunned — Reduces AP available that turn. Stacks. Immune while Indomitable (Sovereign Mind spec).
  • Prone — Penalties to defense and movement. Immune while Indomitable. Many crits inflict Prone.
  • Silence / Muted — Cannot verbalize, use Inspiring abilities, or cast vocal spells. Applied by Throat Slice and Phantom Assassin.
  • Fear — Forces a Fear test. Removed by Sovereign Mind, Blood Rally (Command spec), or Cleric aura.
  • Disordered — Grants your next attack as a Backstab. Applied by Nimble Strike's Shadow Strike spec.
  • Poisoned — Ongoing damage. Primal Aspect (Toxic Adaptation) exploits existing poison for +1d10 wounds.
  • Burn / Disease — DoT effects. Ignored while Unwavering (Unstoppable Force spec) is active.

XP & Progression

Characters advance by spending XP on skills, characteristics, and advanced vocations. There are no fixed classes — every build is constructed from the same pool.

CHARACTERISTIC INCREASE: Cost varies by rank (see Ledger)
SKILL PURCHASE: 50–400 XP depending on vocation tier

Advanced Vocation XP costs:

  • 50 XP — Shield Rush, Deflect, Heavy Arbalest
  • 100 XP — Block, Cleave, Command, Divinity, Expose Armor, Marksman, Metaphysical, Nimble Strike, Opportunist, Phantom Assassin, Sovereign Mind and others
  • 200 XP — Battle-Ready, Concentrate, Deviation, Dodge, Impact, Impale, Infusion of Arcana, Primal Aspect, Throat Slice, Thunderstrike, Tune-up, Unwavering
  • 300 XP — Unwavering (Epic)
  • 400 XP — Rebirth (Epic)

Specializations within a vocation are unlocked automatically when the parent vocation is purchased — they do not cost additional XP.

📈 Efficient Early Progression Tips

  • Invest in your primary characteristic first — it feeds every roll you make.
  • 100 XP skills often provide permanent passive bonuses — prioritize these before 200 XP active skills.
  • Specializations are free with the parent skill — always read the spec before buying to confirm the parent is worth the XP.
  • Epic Vocations (300–400 XP) require specific skill prerequisites — plan your path early or you'll hit a gate.
  • Stacking 3–4 passive wound bonuses from 100 XP skills (Nimble Strike, Expose Armor, Impale, Marksman) is often more efficient than a single 200 XP active skill early on.

Build Archetypes

These are suggested character frameworks — not rigid classes. Mix and match to suit your playstyle.

⚔ The Blademaster (Agility Melee)

Core Stats: Agility (primary), Awareness (secondary)

Key Skills: Nimble Strike, Phantom Assassin, Throat Slice, Impale, Deflect → Revert-Back, Dodge → Jailbreak

Passive Wound Bonus: +5 from passives alone (Nimble +1, Phantom +2, Throat +1, Impale +1) before any roll

Playstyle: Mobile flanker. Gets in fast, deals massive burst from stealth, escapes if surrounded. Dodge and Jailbreak are your escape hatches. Revert-Back turns their crits into your damage.

🪓 The Warbringer (Physique Melee)

Core Stats: Physique (primary), Agility (secondary)

Key Skills: Impact (2H), Cleave → Whirlwind, Sunder → Shatterpoint, Tune-up → Lights-Out, Block → Bulwark

Passive Wound Bonus: +2 from Impact passive + weapon size multiplier (2H = Physique × 2 on active strike)

Playstyle: Frontline destroyer. Cleave handles groups, Sunder strips enemy defenses, Tune-up locks down individual targets. Block/Bulwark makes you a living wall for allies.

🏹 The Deadeye (Ranged)

Core Stats: Awareness (primary), Agility (secondary)

Key Skills: Marksman → Sniper, Opportunist → Seeker, Heavy Arbalest → Linebreaker (crossbow variant)

Passive Wound Bonus: +5 from passives (Marksman +2, Opportunist +1, Arbalest +1 + 2 armor ignore)

Playstyle: Stands back, shoots into any melee without penalty (Opportunist). Sniper spec for 2-AP precision kills. Linebreaker for armor-stripping bolts that punch through targets. Dodge as the only self-defense.

✦ The Arcanist (Spell Damage)

Core Stats: Intellect (primary), Spirit/Willpower (secondary)

Key Skills: Concentrate → Intensify, Metaphysical → Supernatural, Deviation → Divergence

Passive Spell Bonus: +4 (Concentrate +3, Metaphysical +1) before any roll or active skill

Playstyle: Glass cannon. Concentrate gives Advantage to cast and passive +3. Intensify doubles total wounds on success. Divergence splits a single roll across multiple targets. Supernatural spec is effectively a once-per-engagement nuclear option.

🛡 The Bulwark (Tank / WM)

Core Stats: Physique (primary), Willpower (secondary)

Key Skills: Block → Bulwark, Cleric, Sovereign Mind → Indomitable, Unwavering (Epic) → Unstoppable Force

Passive WM Bonus: Armor + Shield + Cleric (+1) + Unwavering (+3) = massive passive floor before any active skills

Playstyle: Absorb everything. Bulwark stance protects an adjacent ally. Indomitable gives a WM spike on demand. Unstoppable Force is the end-game form — near-invincible for one turn. Last Breath keeps you standing at 0 wounds.

🌿 The Druid (Nature / Hybrid)

Core Stats: Willpower (primary), Physique or Agility (secondary)

Key Skills: Primal Aspect → Apex Mutation, Rebirth (Epic) → Seed of Life, Divinity → Sanctity

Passive Bonus: +3 to all Druid healing spells (Rebirth passive). Guardian form: +3 WM, +10 Max Wounds. Predator form: +2 Agility, Bleeding attacks.

Playstyle: Shapeshifter with a safety net. Choose your Aspect based on the encounter. Rebirth is a guaranteed comeback. Seed of Life pulses AoE healing on resurrection. Sanctity for powerful sustained healing when in caster form.
