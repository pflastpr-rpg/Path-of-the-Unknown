Current Situation
Arrival at SunsReach ★ Current Arc
Active

The party arrived at SunsReach at dawn after a five-day journey aboard a Lizardman bone-ship — the fastest vessel any of them have encountered. Deposited 100 feet from the docks by skink oarsmen, they carry a sealed scroll from the Lizardman High Priest addressed to High Cleric Thaddeus of the SunsReach temple.


What just happened — the Sunken Archive:
  • Dresin (Druid, player character) was enthralled by the Aakzad Spirit-Echo broadcast and compelled to open the sealed gateway.
  • Ten Aakzad emerged. Four escorted the party out defensively. Six split east and west with clear purpose into the deep complex.
  • The Lizardman High Priest was furious. He gave the scroll and an ultimatum rather than killing the party outright.
  • The pirate who fled was found dead outside — full of Skink darts. The party's hidden raft was untouched.

Next steps: SunsReach guards at the docks will escort the party to the temple on presentation of the scroll. The city is a decent-sized port — extent unknown to the party. Within the past ten days: the cemetery was desecrated, an artifact stolen from a sealed grave, the head groundsman killed along with two of his men.
Session Log
The Sunken Archive Session 02
Resolved

The party infiltrated the Sunken Archive — a submerged complex of pentagon-shaped chambers sealed by a Lizardman Shamanic lock.


Key Events:
  • The Trance: Dresin heard Spirit-Echo broadcasts escalating through progressive Resist tests (7, 8, then 12 at the door). Enthralled, he spoke the anti-magical counter-lock and the Aakzad resonant language. The gateway opened.
  • The Emergence: Ten sets of green glowing eyes. Four Aakzad stepped forward defensively, speaking: "For your sacrifice we shall now let you take this moment to leave these chambers for we cannot further protect you from the others once they arrive."
  • The Split: Six Aakzad divided into two groups of three, moving east and west into the deeper complex with evident purpose.
  • The Confrontation: 20 Lizardmen, 40 Skinks, 1 Skink High Priest surrounding the entrance. Pirate ship sailing off. One pirate dead, filled with darts. A Lizardman bone-ship offshore.
  • The High Priest: "Your ignorance and weak will has unleashed a great evil upon these lands." Delivered the scroll. Threatened death if the party refuses the mission.
