The Overcast Menu (+1 AP each)

  • 🔥 Empower: +1d10 + Primary Stat (Wounds/Heal).
  • 🎯 Precision: +2 Bonus to the roll.
  • 🛡️ Piercing: Ignore 3 points of Mitigation.
  • 📏 Enlarge: +10ft AoE or +1 Size.
  • ⏳ Extend: Increase duration by +1 Turn.
  • 🏹 Vantage: Increase max range by +50 Feet.
  • 📣 Echo: Apply buff to +1 additional target.

Passive: Arcane Mastery

At 15 Points in a magic skill, add your Spirit points to all wounds/healing.
