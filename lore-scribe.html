How It Works

Write your entry here. Hit Commit to Tome. The entry is saved to your browser's local storage.

Open lore.html (the Tome) in the same browser. Scribed entries appear automatically under the correct alphabetical section, marked with a Scribed badge.

If the Tome is already open in another tab, it updates live when you commit.

Export JSON backs up all your entries to a file. Import JSON restores them — useful if you change browser or device.

The Section Headers field supports simple formatting: start a line with ## to create a section header, followed by the text for that section.
