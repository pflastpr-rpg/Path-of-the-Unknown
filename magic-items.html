Relics & Artifacts

"Forged in forgotten eras, bought with blood."

Powerful enchantments warp the very weave of reality. These magical armaments and trinkets defy the standard rules of combat, granting their wielders devastating advantages.

INDEX OF

Magic Items

Mace of Disruption TYPE: Blunt Weapon | BONUS: +1 Magical

When a creature of evil is struck by this mace, they must make a Willpower test based upon the wielder’s Faith skill. If failed, the target suffers 2d10 x 2 + melee bonus wounds.

If the creature is Undead and fails the test, they additionally suffer the effects of the Turn Undead spell. This applies to each target successfully struck with the mace.

Pearl of Power TYPE: Trinket / Focus

This item allows the wizard to cast an additional spell that is not currently memorized. This prevents the character from having to cast a spell not already memorized with the increased TN penalty.

The caster may choose any spell available to them from the base wizard spells and any spell in an advanced path.

Boccob's Blessed Book TYPE: Wondrous Tome

A durable, waterproof, and locked tome that can hold up to 45 spells. Simply possessing the book provides a +1 bonus to any Willpower test.

If the wizard wields the book in place of a weapon and reads directly from it while casting an already known spell, the selected spell's TN is lowered by 1. For any non-memorized spell cast while reading from the book, the standard penalty to the TN is removed altogether.

Ardent Crusader TYPE: Edged Weapon | BONUS: +3 Magical (Conditional)

In the hands of any character other than a Crusader, this holy sword will perform only as a standard +2 Sword.

In the hands of a Crusader, however, it creates a magic resistance aura granting +2 Wound Mitigation vs spells in a 5-foot radius. Furthermore, it grants a +1 bonus to any Willpower test in a 5-foot radius, and inflicts Normal + Bonus + Light-Soul points of damage upon chaotic evil opponents (Beasts of Chaos, Daemonic creatures and their followers, Undead).

Ring of Spell Turning TYPE: Jewelry

If a spell is cast upon the wearer (excluding AoE or Touch spells), the wearer must roll against the caster’s To-Hit result. The ring provides a +2 bonus to the wearer's roll.

  • Equal Result: If the result is exactly equal to the caster’s success, the spell is simply dispelled, affecting no one.
  • Higher Result: If the wearer gains a minimum of 1 higher on their result, the spell is immediately turned back onto the casting wizard.
